## What I document

For each activity, I aim to record scope, my methodology, observations, security impact, remediation and evidence. I distinguish guided learning from tasks completed independently. I do not claim certifications or professional experience that I cannot verify.

## Responsible practice

All security testing is restricted to authorised training environments or systems for which I have explicit permission. Reports omit credentials, session tokens and unnecessarily detailed challenge solutions. Educational lab findings are not claims about real-world organisations.

## Portfolio direction

Over time, this repository will contain learning notes, authorised lab reports and examples of professional security writing. Substantial original scripts will be maintained as separate repositories and linked here after they are ready.
